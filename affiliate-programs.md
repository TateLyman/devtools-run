These are the highest-earning programs. Crypto exchanges pay lifetime (or long-term) commissions on every trade your referrals make.

- **Requirements**: Content creators, traders, influencers with qualifying website/app/community

- **Can sign up now**: YES

## TIER 2: SOLANA-SPECIFIC PROGRAMS

### 7. Jupiter Referral Program (Solana DEX)
- **Commission**: Custom fee up to 1% on swaps (you set the %). Jupiter takes only 2.5% of your fee
- **Signup URL**: https://referral.jup.ag/
- **Docs**: https://dev.jup.ag/tool-kits/referral-program
- **GitHub**: https://github.com/TeamRaccoons/referral
- **Integration**: API-based. Add your referral account to Jupiter swap API calls
- **How it works**:
  1. Create a referral account at referral.jup.ag
  2. Set your platform fee in basis points (e.g., 20 bps = 0.2%)
  3. Pass your referral account in Jupiter API swap requests
  4. Earn fees on every swap routed through your integration
- **Can sign up now**: YES
- **Where to embed**: **This is the #1 priority** -- integrate into the Telegram bot's swap functionality. Every swap the bot routes through Jupiter can earn fees.
- **Code example**: Pass `platformFeeBps` and `feeAccount` params in Jupiter API calls
